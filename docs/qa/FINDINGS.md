## Ranked bugs

### 1. HIGH: reward-bearing groups: every second transaction fails on chain (footprint)
- Repro: `qa/flows/21-repro-footprint.ts`: 6 supplies in a rewards group, the 2nd, 4th and 6th FAIL ([d78e0479](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/d78e047936efac7a7df9f9e33453bd3aeb0a2b7656d77f7ef101c1e518d0c8c3), [4a03763e](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/4a03763eba033b7975e2cb8eb12d0a979f3af8762ced26f9a9f6f7e4b802fb51)); the same in a group without rewards passes 4/4. Also hit withdraw ([0d90e192](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/0d90e1926e74b19e5f41c0027302592ab87b7fda9215253245dada3bcc5f8360)) and margin `transfer_margin_to_spot` ([9cfb04f1](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/9cfb04f1a04eb499723cfcb99ee61caf5be3fbb08967af24beb832955b8fd0fc)). `diag-tx.ts` shows `storage exceeded_limit: trying to access contract data key outside of the footprint ... SupplyIndex(market)`.
- Cause: `core/simple-peridottroller/src/contract.rs:3829-3871` writes `SupplyIndex`/`SupplyIndexTime` only when `dt_s > 0`. A simulation that lands in the same ledger close time as the previous accrual (dt = 0) records the key read-only; execution one ledger later writes it and traps. Any user's previous interaction with that market can trigger it, not just your own.
- App side (`app/src/lib/peridot/rpc.ts:109-157`, `group-client.ts:164`): the app simulates once, signs, submits and reports only "The transaction failed on chain." The user pays the fee and gets no cause. Mainnet group 2 is affected.
- Fix: in `submitContractLocked`, if the simulated tx touches a reward-bearing market, wait for a ledger after the last confirmed tx (as `margin.ts` `write()` already does with `waitForLedgerBeyond`), and on a `FAILED` result with a `trapped`/footprint diagnostic re-simulate and offer one automatic retry; fetch and show the diagnostic error. Contract fix (Lead A): always write `SupplyIndexTime`/`BorrowIndexTime` (touch the key even when dt = 0) so the footprint is stable.

### 2. HIGH (mainnet tomorrow): Borrow on a DeFindex/Blend-routed market fails closed when the boosted health cache is older than 5 minutes
- Evidence: mainnet dry run, group 1 XLM `borrow` (even 0.0001 XLM) simulates to `Error(WasmVm, InvalidAction)`; `BoostedHealthCache` age is 9,921 s (group 3: 27,078 s). Testnet proof `qa/flows/23-yield-borrow-stale.ts`: borrow right after supply PASS, after 5.5 min FAIL, after `refresh_boosted_underlying()` ([18668763](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/18668763f247e3765fd9af8ff6d1ec1d1a4b6eab3ee81e5ec1c3b6d5f60e4edf) hash prefix in RESULTS) PASS.
- Cause: `core/receipt-vault/src/contract.rs:322-362` (`boosted health cache stale`, 5 min) and the permissionless `refresh_boosted_underlying` at line 1451. The app never calls it (`group-client.ts:156-161` Borrow; also withdraw-with-debt). The keeper that does call it is not live.
- Good news: the margin open does NOT need it (`borrow_for_margin` skips the health cache): `qa/flows/24-margin-yield.ts` opened and closed a 2x long on a DeFindex-routed borrowed market with a stale cache. So tomorrow's 2x open on group 1 should not hit this, but the plain Borrow button on group 1 XLM and group 3 will.
- Fix: before a Borrow (and before a withdraw while in debt) on a market with a route, read `BoostedHealthCache.updated_at` and, if older than about 4 minutes, send `refresh_boosted_underlying()` first (about 5.3M instructions, 20 entries, fee about 0.004 XLM) and say so in the UI; map the failure to "Market valuation is stale, refresh needed".

### 3. MEDIUM (contract, for Lead A): `sync_pairs` caps both sides of a pair with the margin asset's collateral factor
- `contracts/margin-factory/src/lib.rs:1003-1008` (`capped_leverage(env, rec, a, ...)` used for Long and Short). For a Long the collateral is the base asset `b`. Repro `qa/flows/42-repro-pairs.ts`: after USDT CF drops to 40%, `get_perps_pair_config(XLM, USDT, Long)` stays 3 while `effective_max_leverage(USDT)` is 1. `qa/flows/43-repro-collateral-cf.ts` then opened a 3x long by raw calls ([777315a6](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/777315a6979d0ae59ac6) prefix; activate succeeded, health 7.37). The app's `quoteOpen` blocks it (`margin.ts:180-181`, uses `effective_max_leverage` of the collateral market), but the chain does not. Fix: cap Long pairs by `b`'s CF and Short pairs by `a`'s CF, or cap by the min of both.

### 4. MEDIUM: the app cannot run on testnet
- `app/src/lib/peridot/deployments/testnet.json` is the placeholder `{"status":"Awaiting Lead A deployment manifest"}`, so `getDeployments()` throws with `NEXT_PUBLIC_NETWORK=testnet`; `scripts/sync-deployments.mjs testnet` is blocked by a missing `[A] READY: testnet phase-1` marker, and the root `deployments/testnet.json` has no `external` block (Reflector, XLM, router ids). The launchpad pages are meant to read testnet. The harness overlays it in memory. Fix: publish a testnet manifest with `external` (ids in `qa/lib/bootstrap.ts` `TESTNET_EXTERNAL`).

### 5. MEDIUM: margin open moves funds before it knows the open can succeed
- `margin.ts:202-229`: `transfer_spot_to_margin` is signed first; `begin_open_position_v3` then panics (`UnreachableCodeReached`, shown as `Error(WasmVm, InvalidAction)`) if the pool quote is outside `max_open_deviation_scaled` (10%) of the oracle. Testnet pool was 13% off Reflector, so every open failed after the transfer and the user's 100 XLM sat as free margin (`testnet-margin-prealign.json`; recovery via "Return margin" worked, tx [6e290d58](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/6e290d58d13dbf10b744) prefix). `quoteOpen` has all inputs (execution config, both prices, pool quote). Fix: compute the band check in `quoteOpen` and refuse with a readable message before any signature. Environment note: to test I moved the shared testnet pool by selling 12,000 XLM into it (`qa/flows/05-pool-align.ts`); it is now about 5% off.

### 6. MEDIUM: chain errors reach the user as raw codes
- Client guards exist for CF, caps, buffer, delay, but not for: supply over the supply cap and supply/borrow into a paused market (`Error(WasmVm, InvalidAction)` plus an event log dump), duplicate token in `addControlMarket` (`Error(Contract, #6)`), applying a staged margin incentive before its ETA (`control-client.ts:259-274` checks only market proposals; raw InvalidAction), and `submitContractLocked` swallows the result code of a FAILED tx (`rpc.ts:150-153`). Same on mainnet read errors: `/groups/99` renders "Unable to read get_group: HostError: Error(Contract, #4) Event log ..." and overflows the 375 px viewport (`docs/qa/screens/dark-mobile-groups_99.png`). Fix: a code table (#4 group not found, #6 duplicate market, #20 CF out of range, #22 zero cap, #23 idle buffer, #24 reserve factor, #44 staged delay, #50 rewards locks markets), pre-check caps and pause flags in `lend()`, truncate diagnostic text.

### 7. LOW
- `docs/DEMO-MAINNET.md` says the rewards speed 200 stroops/s is "about 17 XLM per day"; it is 1.728 XLM/day (the app correctly shows 1.72 and a 12 day runway). The page truncates 1.728 to 1.72.
- `lend()` with `Enable collateral` on an already-entered market still builds a tx (sim OK, no writes); harmless but costs a signature.
- Raw `deposit()` without `enter_market` is accepted on chain in a rewards group (the "enter first" rule is client-only); shares supplied that way earn nothing until re-entered.
- `transfer_ownership` is disabled by design (message correct).
- Launch wizard `description` is saved only in the browser (UI says so).

## Mainnet dry-run verdict for tomorrow (funded wallet, real signatures)
- Works: enter_market, supply and withdraw XLM on groups 1, 2, 3; claim on group 2 (if accrued > 0 and the wallet holds the reward trustline, XLM needs none); repay; all Control Panel actions for the owner; add market (EURC) and create_group; margin quote and the first stage (`transfer_spot_to_margin`, fee about 0.18 XLM).
- Will fail or needs a step: Borrow on group 1 XLM or group 3 (bug 2: refresh first, or run the keeper); anything USDC needs a USDC trustline and USDC balance (group 1 USDC has zero liquidity, so shorts are impossible); in group 2 any action sent within the same ledger of another user's interaction can fail on chain (bug 1), just retry after 10 s.
- Resource headroom: maximum 87 ledger entries (limit 200 reads), maximum 49.9M instructions (limit 400M). Fees: supply 0.007 XLM, withdraw 0.008 XLM, add_market 1.0 XLM, create_group 0.44 XLM.
- For the 2x long: margin XLM, base USDC needs the pool inside the oracle band (check `quoteOpen` succeeds before signing; the app does not check the band itself, bug 5) and enough XLM liquidity (30 XLM supplied by the deployer).
